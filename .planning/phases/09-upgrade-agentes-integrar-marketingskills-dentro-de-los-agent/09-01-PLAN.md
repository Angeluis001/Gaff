## Product Overview
GAFF All Fishing is a premium sport fishing charter company operating out of Cabo San Lucas marina, Baja California Sur, México. ## Target Audience
**Primary:** US tourists aged 35–55 — couples celebrating anniversaries, families with teens, groups of friends, corporate team outings. Annual household income $100k+. Budget $1,000–$5,000 per trip. Books 3–8 weeks in advance, researches on TripAdvisor and Google before booking.

**Psychographic:** They want an adventure story to tell. The fish is secondary — the experience, the photo with the marlin, the bonding moment is the product. They fear wasting a vacation day on a subpar charter.

## Problems & Pain Points
- **Trust uncertainty:** "How do I know this charter is legit and not a tourist trap?"
- **Season uncertainty:** "Is October/my travel date actually good for fishing in Cabo?"
- **Choice paralysis:** "Which boat size fits my group and budget?"
- **Booking friction:** "I don't want to DM a stranger — can I book and pay online?"
- **Last-minute anxiety:** "What if I show up and conditions are bad?"

## Competitive Landscape
- **Pisces Sport Fishing** (piscessportfishing.com): Largest fleet in Cabo, celebrity clients, higher price point. Weakness: impersonal, volume-focused.
- **Picante Fleet**: Well-known, mid-market. Weakness: older fleet, less digital presence.
- **Travel agencies**: Mark up GAFF and competitor rates 20–40%. GAFF's direct booking cuts this.
- **GAFF advantage:** Personalized service, real-time online booking, IGFA certified captains, 4.8★ TripAdvisor rating, transparent pricing.

## Brand Voice
**Confident, expert, warm — never cheap, never pushy, never generic tourist-trap language.**
- Say: "Our captains know these waters. October marlin season is the peak — and spots are filling."
- Don't say: "Great fishing fun for the whole family! Book now!!!"
- Tone on WhatsApp: direct friend who knows fishing, not a sales bot
- Tone in email: knowledgeable concierge, not a mass marketer

## Proof Points
- 4.8★ average on TripAdvisor (500+ reviews)
- 15+ years operating out of Cabo San Lucas marina
- IGFA-certified captains
- Catch-and-release certified for billfish (IGFA & GrayFishTag)
- 500+ charters per year
- Direct online booking with transparent pricing — no agency markup

## Fishing Seasons (Cabo San Lucas)
| Species | Peak Season | Best Month |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Blue Marlin | Jun–Nov | October |
| Yellowfin Tuna | May–Dec | Aug–Sep |
| Dorado (Mahi-Mahi) | Jun–Oct | Jul–Sep |
| Wahoo | Jul–Nov | Year-round |
| Roosterfish | May–Aug | Coastal |

## Conversion Triggers (apply in all agent outputs)
- Scarcity: "October dates filling fast — marlin peak"
- Social proof: "4.8★ from 500+ verified anglers"
- Loss aversion: "Miss the marlin peak window and wait another year"
- Goal-gradient: Closer the preferred date, lower the activation energy needed
- Reciprocity: Free fishing reports and season guidance build trust before ask
